Tell time in bits. Binary-Clock turns your background into a binary clock – on your home screen, your lock screen and as a widget.

SEVEN STYLES – EACH IN LIGHT AND DARK
• Classic – one column of dots per digit
• Binary – hours, minutes and seconds as true binary numbers
• LED – square light-emitting diodes in a case
• Paper – a calm look with red seconds
• Rings – three rings for hours, minutes and seconds
• Terminal – ones and zeros like on the command line
• Bars – minimalist strokes

MAKE IT YOURS
• Light, dark or automatic, following your system
• Pick any accent color
• Drag and pinch to place and size the clock – it snaps to the center
• Set up home screen and lock screen completely independently
• Show or hide seconds, date and the reading aid

WIDGET
The binary clock as a widget for your home screen – in any size.

READING AID
New to binary clocks? The reading aid also shows the time or the place values as numbers. After a few days you'll read the bits without it.

HOW TO READ IT
Every active bit has a value – 1, 2, 4, 8, and in true binary mode also 16 and 32. Add up the active bits in a column, row or ring and you have the number.
