Tell time in bits. Binary-Clock turns your background into a glowing binary clock – on your home screen, your lock screen and as a widget.

FOUR STYLES
• Classic – one column of dots per digit (BCD), warm amber on black
• Binary – hours, minutes and seconds as true binary numbers
• LED – square light-emitting diodes in a case, with digits underneath
• Paper – a light, calm look with red seconds

MAKE IT YOURS
• Pick any accent color
• Show or hide seconds, date and the reading aid
• On the lock screen, the clock moves below the system clock automatically

READING AID
New to binary clocks? The reading aid also shows the time or the place values as numbers. After a few days you'll read the dots without it.

LIGHT ON BATTERY
The live wallpaper only draws while it's visible. The widget updates once a minute.

PRIVATE
No ads, no accounts, no data collection, no internet connection.

How to read it: every lit dot has a value – 1, 2, 4, 8 (and 16 and 32 in true binary mode). Add up the lit dots in a column or row and you have the number.
